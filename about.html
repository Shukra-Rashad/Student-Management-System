What Is a Student Management System?

A Student Management System is a web-based application designed to help manage student information. It provides a simple way to enter student details and helps keep information organised.

Student Registration

Enter student details through a simple registration form.

Form Validation

Check the information entered to help prevent incomplete or incorrectly formatted details.

Data Management
